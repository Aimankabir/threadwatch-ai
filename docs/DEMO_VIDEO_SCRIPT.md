## Recording tips

- **Clean tab** — close all other tabs and bookmarks. Disable notifications.
- **Use a real voice** — record Bangla with your phone, narrate English over it.
- **Zoom 110 %** — makes text legible on judges' laptop screens.
- **Speed it up 1.5× in post** — but keep voice-over at 1× so it's understandable.
- **No music** — judges mute by default. Clean narration wins.
