# ThreadWatch AI — Concept Note

## 1. Idea
**ThreadWatch AI** is an AI-powered workforce intelligence and compliance platform for Bangladesh's $46B ready-made-garment (RMG) industry. It turns every anonymous worker complaint into a structured, explainable, predictive signal — and gives brands, buyers, and inspectors a real-time health score for every factory under their watch.

## 2. Stakeholders
- **Garment workers** — 4 million+ across Bangladesh, the primary data source(Ai generated). Not a real time data source.
- **Factory managers** — need prioritized action, not alert fatigue.
- **BGMEA / Accord / ILO Better Work inspectors** — need cross-factory visibility.
- **Global brand buyers** (H&M, Zara, Levi's CSR teams) — need verifiable compliance before sourcing.
- **Department of Inspection for Factories & Establishments (DIFE)** — needs real-time red-flag reporting.

## 3. Real-World Problem
Three problems converge in Bangladesh's RMG compliance today:
1. **Workers can't safely report** — fear of retaliation is real; paper-based grievance boxes are routinely ignored.
2. **Managers react late** — generic dashboards show *numbers going up*, not *which factory is about to fail*.
3. **Buyers can't verify** — audit reports are months old by the time they reach a sourcing decision.

## 4. Proposed Solution
ThreadWatch AI provides three roles on one platform:
- **Worker view** — voice or text input in Bangla; auto-stripped of names, phones, and IDs; each report gets a 16-character fingerprint so the worker can verify *their report is anonymous*.
- **Manager view** — a single factory health score that is **fully explainable** ("why this score? → 3 critical open + 12 unresolved + 18% resolution rate"). Click any number to see the reasoning.
- **Inspector view** — cross-factory anomaly detection. Every 12 seconds, the AI detects a fresh risk pattern.
## 5. Effectiveness & Key Merits
- **Built for trust**: provenance hashing, explainability, and a live anomaly pulse make it impossible to claim the AI is "a black box."
- **Voice-first**: works for workers who can't or won't type — designed for Bangla, ready for Swahili, Hindi, and Vietnamese.
- **Predictive**: 30-day forward risk forecasting built on the same complaint stream.
- **Open & inspectable**: pure HTML/CSS/JS, deployable as a static site, no backend lock-in.

**One sentence**: *the AI listens, tells the truth, and shows what's coming.*
